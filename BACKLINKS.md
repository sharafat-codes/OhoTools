## Etiquette (don't get banned or flagged)
- Lead with value: recommend the **specific tool** that solves the poster's problem, not "check out my site."
- One genuine post per community; don't cross-post the same thing to 10 subreddits in a day.
- **Deep-link exact tool pages** — spreads link equity across the site, not just the homepage.
- Space submissions out over days; don't blast 20 directories in an hour.

## Track results
- New referring domains: **Bing Webmaster → Backlinks** and **GSC → Links**.
- Re-check **GSC average position** in 3–4 weeks — links + time should pull it off page 6.
